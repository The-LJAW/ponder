Don't skip the video.
Understand it.

Paste a YouTube or Vimeo link. Ponder gives you a summary, an outline, flashcards, and five questions that make you think about what you watched.

Five kinds of questions, every video

  • Apply How would you use this in your own life or work?
  • Connect What does it link to that you already know?
  • Push back Where is the argument weakest?
  • Transfer Does the idea survive in a different domain?
  • Decide Faced with a real choice, which way would you go?

Not quiz questions checking whether you watched. The flashcards handle recall; these handle understanding.
