Ponder

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

What Ponder sends to its server

When you ask Ponder to study a video, the web app or Chrome extension sends the video's link and a random install ID to the Ponder server. Nothing else. The extension only looks at the address of your current tab when that tab is on YouTube or Vimeo, and it only sends that address when you press "Ponder this video".

What the server does with it

The server fetches the video's public transcript through a transcript provider (Supadata), sends the transcript to Anthropic's Claude API to write the study pack, and returns the pack to you. Study packs are cached by video for up to 30 days so the same video loads instantly for the next person. The random install ID and your IP address are used only to count daily free uses and expire after two days.

What stays on your device

Your answers to the reflection questions and your history of study packs are stored only in your browser. They are never sent to the server. Clearing your browser data or uninstalling the extension deletes them.

What Ponder does not do

No accounts, no ads, no tracking scripts, no selling or sharing of data. Ponder does not read your browsing history, page contents, or anything on sites other than YouTube and Vimeo.
